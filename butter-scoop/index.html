    Old History. Brand New Scoops.
    For centuries, ice cream has been a global obsession. Today, Butter & Scoop is reinventing
    that classic love affair right in the heart of Fort Lee, NJ. Nestled at 250 Main St, we bridge the gap
    between the bakery and the creamery. By stuffing flaky, buttery pastries with gourmet ice cream
    and loading them with spectacular toppings, we’ve created a mouth-watering destination that is destined
    to become a local legend.

    A brand-new legend in New Jersey, crafted for pure indulgence and just as delicious and mouth-watering today.

    Pure Fresh Milk

    We use only natural, fresh and local (when possible) ingredients in our gelato.

    No Preservatives
