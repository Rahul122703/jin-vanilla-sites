About Our Restaurant

Since 1986, our family has been serving authentic Greek food to Cliffside Park. We first opened our doors as It's Greek to Me, and for more than three decades we shared the recipes and hospitality we grew up with.

After 34 years, we gave our home a new name and a fresh look: Mythos Greek Taverna. The renovation brought a bright island-inspired dining room and an expanded menu with even more traditional Greek dishes. What didn't change is what matters most: the same family, the same recipes, and the same welcome.

Our kitchen offers both traditional Greek classics and contemporary Greek dishes, with something for every appetite and dietary preference. Everything on our menu is made in house, every day. No shortcuts.

When you visit, you're not just a customer. You're family. Come hungry, stay a while, and let us take care of the rest.
