1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as information automatically collected when you browse the Site.

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and physical delivery address if/when you place an order, make a reservation, or join our mailing list.
  • Payment Data: Credit card numbers and billing details processed securely through our third-party payment processors. We do not store raw financial data on our servers.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, and page-view data collected automatically via cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to provide a seamless experience, specifically to:

  1. Process and deliver online orders and/or services.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business which may include:

  • Online Ordering & Ecommerce Partners: To fulfill fulfillment logistics.

  • Marketing Emails: You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of our emails.

  • Cookie Preferences: You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies, though some site functions (like keeping items in an online cart) may fail to work properly.

5. State-Specific Privacy Rights (US)

Depending on your state of residence (e.g., California, Virginia, Colorado), you may have specific rights regarding your personal data. These include the right to request access to the data we hold, request deletion of your information, or correct inaccuracies. To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@example.com.

6. Cookie Notice

Our Site uses cookies and tracking technologies to differentiate you from other users. This helps us provide a smooth browsing experience and allows us to improve our digital services.

Types of Cookies We Use

  1. Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for navigating the website and using features like the online ordering cart and secure reservation checkout. Without these, the website cannot function.
  2. Performance & Analytics Cookies: Collect information anonymously about how visitors interact with our menus and pages (e.g., Google Analytics). This tells us which dishes are viewed most frequently.
  3. Functionality Cookies: Remember choices you make (such as your preferred location or dietary preference indicators) to tailor a more personalized experience.
  4. Targeting & Marketing Cookies: Placed by third-party advertising networks or reservation widgets to track conversion rates from social media advertisements.

How to Control Cookies

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser setting to decline them if you prefer. Alternatively, you can change your preferences via our website's Cookie Settings widget at any time.
